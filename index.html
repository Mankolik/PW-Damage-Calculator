Calculation notes

  • Armor reduces only physical/base damage. If armor would reduce the physical portion to zero or less, Pixel Worlds displays 1 final base damage.
  • Elemental damage ignores armor, is reduced only by the matching elemental resistance, and never critical strikes.
  • For this baseline, total-damage perks are multiplied together and then applied to the physical/base weapon damage before armor. Melee Madness applies only to weapons marked as melee; guns, throwable shields, thrown weapons, crossbows, cannons, and staffs are marked as ranged.

Mechanics reference Pixel Worlds Wiki pages for Damage, Armor, Perks, and element behavior. Weapon and enemy cards link directly to their matching Pixel Worlds Wiki pages.
